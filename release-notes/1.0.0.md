First public release.

## Install
1. Download **PixelCrew-1.0.0.dmg** below.
2. Open it and drag PixelCrew to Applications.
3. Open PixelCrew and connect your agents on the **Set up your crew** screen: sign in with your Claude, ChatGPT, Google or xAI subscription, or paste an API key (kept in your macOS Keychain).

Requires macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## What's in it
- Claude Code, Codex, Gemini (Antigravity CLI) and Grok Build plan, split, build and cross-review work as one team in a live pixel-art office
- Senior and worker roles, with strong, standard and fast model tiers per job
- Quota balancing that moves work away from an agent that is running out of its limit
- Ask mode for read-only questions about your project
- English and Turkish
