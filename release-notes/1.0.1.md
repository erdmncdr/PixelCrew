A new app icon.

- PixelCrew has a new icon: a ring of glossy pixel tiles in the four agents' colours (Claude, Codex, Gemini, Grok) around a 2x2 core, one tile per agent.
- The toolbar logo, the browser tab icon and the loading screen use the same four-tile mark.

## Install
Download **PixelCrew-1.0.1.dmg** below, open it and drag PixelCrew to Applications. If you already have 1.0.0, just replace it; your settings and history stay. Requires macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
